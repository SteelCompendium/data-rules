name: Catch Breath
scc: mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.trait.common.maneuvers/catch-breath
type: trait
---

A creature who uses the [Catch Breath](catch-breath.md) maneuver spends a Recovery and regains Stamina equal to their recovery value. (See below for Stamina. See Recoveries in Chapter 1: [The Basics](../../../../chapter/the-basics.md).)

A creature who is dying (see Dying and Death in Stamina below) can't use the [Catch Breath](catch-breath.md) maneuver, but other creatures can help them spend Recoveries in other ways.
