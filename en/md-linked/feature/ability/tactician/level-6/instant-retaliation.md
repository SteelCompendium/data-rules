- **≤11:** A < WEAK, [dazed](../../../../condition/dazed.md) (save ends)
- **12-16:** A < AVERAGE, [dazed](../../../../condition/dazed.md) (save ends)
- **17+:** A < STRONG, [dazed](../../../../condition/dazed.md) (save ends)
