Many tests are maneuvers if made in combat. Searching a chest with a [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md) test, picking a door's lock with an [Agility](../../../rule/character/agility.md) test, or lifting a portcullis with a [Might](../../../rule/character/might.md) test would all be maneuvers. Assisting a test is also a maneuver in combat (see Assist a Test in Chapter 9: [Tests](../../../chapter/tests.md)).

Complex or time-consuming tests might require a main action if made in combat—or could take so long that they can't be made during combat at all. Other tests that take no time at all, such as a [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md) test to recall lore about mummies, are usually free maneuvers in combat. The Director has the final say regarding which tests can be made as maneuvers.
