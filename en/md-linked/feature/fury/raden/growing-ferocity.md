As your ferocity grows, you gain benefits as noted on the [Raden](../../../kit/raden.md) [Growing Ferocity](../boren/growing-ferocity.md) table. Benefits are cumulative except where an improved benefit replaces a lesser benefit.

## Raden Growing Ferocity Table

| Ferocity        | Benefit                                                                                                                                           |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 2               | Whenever you use the [Disengage](../../common/move-actions/disengage.md) move action, the distance you can [shift](../../../movement/shifting.md) gains a bonus equal to your Agility score.                                 |
| 4               | The first time you [shift](../../../movement/shifting.md) on a turn, you gain 1 [surge](../../../rule/resource/surge.md).                                                                                             |
| 6               | You gain an edge on Agility tests, the [Escape Grab](../../common/maneuvers/escape-grab.md) maneuver, and the [Knockback](../../common/maneuvers/knockback.md) maneuver.                                                          |
| 8 (4th level)   | The first time you [shift](../../../movement/shifting.md) on a turn, you gain 2 [surges](../../../rule/resource/surge.md) instead of 1.                                                                               |
| 10 (7th level)  | You have a double edge on Agility tests, the [Escape Grab](../../common/maneuvers/escape-grab.md) maneuver, and the [Knockback](../../common/maneuvers/knockback.md) maneuver                                                     |
| 12 (10th level) | Whenever you use a heroic ability, you gain 10 [temporary Stamina](../../../rule/health/temporary-stamina.md). Additionally, the potency of any effects targeting you is reduced by 2 for you. |
