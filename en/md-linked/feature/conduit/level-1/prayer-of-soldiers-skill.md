Your god gives your mind the training of a soldier. You can wear light armor and wield light weapons effectively, even though you don't have a kit. While you wear light armor, you gain a +3 bonus to [Stamina](../../../rule/health/stamina.md), and that bonus increases by 3 at 4th, 7th, and 10th levels. While you wield a light weapon, you gain a +1 damage bonus with weapon abilities, including [free strikes](../../common/main-actions/free-strike.md). You can use light armor treasures and light weapon treasures.

If you have a kit, you can't take this blessing.
