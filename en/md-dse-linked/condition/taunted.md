A creature who is [taunted](taunted.md) has a double [bane](../rule/dice/bane.md) on ability rolls for any ability that doesn't target the creature who [taunted](taunted.md) them, as long as they have line of effect to that creature. If a creature gains the [taunted](taunted.md) condition from one source while already [taunted](taunted.md) by a different source, the new condition replaces the old one.
